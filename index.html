FAQ

Good to know.

Do I need any experience to start? +

None at all. Padel is famously easy to pick up — most players are rallying within their first session. Our coaches meet you exactly where you are.

What should I bring to my first session? +

Just comfortable sportswear and clean court shoes. We provide premium rackets and balls for every first session — turn up and play.

Can I come alone or do I need a partner? +

Come exactly as you are. We'll match you into a friendly group or pair you with a coach — many lifelong friendships started right here.

How do I book and what does it cost? +

Use the form below to request your first session. We'll reach out with availability and a tailored introductory rate within 24 hours.
