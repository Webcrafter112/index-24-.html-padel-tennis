# Hero video

Drop your cinematic golden-hour reel here as `hero.mp4` (and optionally `hero.webm`).

The landing page probes for `assets/video/hero.mp4` at runtime:
- If present, it lazy-loads and fades in as the fullscreen hero background.
- If absent, an elegant Ken-Burns image fallback is shown automatically.

Suggested storyboard for the reel:
1. A professional female padel player walking confidently onto the court at golden hour.
2. Slow-motion close-ups of the racket, ball, shoes and hands.
3. A powerful serve.
4. A dynamic rally.
5. Ball impacts with cinematic particles.
6. The winning point.
7. A slow smile looking into the camera.
8. Sunset and logo reveal.

Recommended: 1920×1080, H.264, ~8–15s loop, muted, optimized < 6 MB for fast loading.
